## Topic Selected: AI Perception Drift

**Why this topic:**

After researching the latest SEO/AI search trends for late March 2026, the most compelling and timely angle is **"AI Perception Drift"** — the concept that AI systems are constantly forming, updating, and reinforcing impressions of brands based on the data they consume, and that these impressions (encoded in AI models) directly influence how brands are represented in AI-generated answers.

This topic is uniquely differentiated from existing portfolio articles:
- **topic79** (AI Citation Optimization): Covered being cited by AI → this topic goes deeper: it's about the *accumulated impression* AI forms, not just individual citations
- **topic189** (Zero-Click Economy): Covered zero-click visibility → this topic covers what happens *inside* the AI model's knowledge
- **topic200** (Entity SEO & AI Agents): Covered entity SEO for AI agents → this topic adds the "training data" and "Perception Drift" layer
- **topic165** (Agentic GEO): Covered getting called by AI agents → this topic covers the upstream question: what the AI *believes* about your brand before it calls you

The topic is timely because:
1. Google March 2026 Core Update went live on March 27, 2026
2. AI systems are increasingly persistent in their brand models
3. The concept of "Perception Drift" as a measurable phenomenon is just emerging in SEO discourse
4. It's highly actionable with clear 5-strategy framework

## Key Themes Covered

1. **What is Perception Drift** — AI's "mental model" of a brand, how it forms, and why it matters
2. **The March 2026 Core Update** — Why this update makes Perception Drift management more urgent
3. **Five pathways that drive Perception Drift**:
   - Content quality in AI-trained data sources
   - Sentiment and context of brand mentions
   - Comparative positioning against competitors
   - Entity signal consistency across platforms
   - Recency and update frequency
4. **Measurement approaches** — AI answer auditing, citation analysis, brand mention monitoring, entity signal audits
5. **Five actionable strategies** — Original research, comparative content ownership, Schema markup, citation networks, quarterly AI impression reviews
6. **The virtuous vs vicious cycle** connecting the March 2026 update and Perception Drift
7. **30-day action plan** — Week-by-week implementation guide
