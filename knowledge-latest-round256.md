### What Changed on March 24, 2026

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|

- Tencent News articles

**Implication:** Brand content must be published on WeChat Official Accounts to be eligible for WeChat AI Search citations. External web content is secondary in this ecosystem.

### Immediate Implications

## Deep Dive: Findings #4-10 (Condensed)

### Finding #4 — a16z GEO Publication: Institutional Validation

Andreessen Horowitz published "How Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Rewrites the Rules of Search" in May 2025. By April 2026, this framework has become the **institutional reference point** for enterprise GEO strategy.

The a16z framework introduced several concepts now mainstream:
- **GEO ≠ SEO**: SEO targets ranking position; GEO targets citation probability
- **Authoritativeness > Optimization**: AI engines select sources based on perceived expertise, not keyword density
- **The "LLM-native content" concept**: Content designed from inception for AI consumption, not humans
- **The citation network effect**: Being cited by AI-preferred sources increases your own citation probability

### Finding #5 — GEO Verification Science: AI Re-Verification of Its Own Citations

A new dimension of GEO science: AI engines now **re-verify the accuracy of their own citations** before including them in answers.

This means:
- **Accuracy consistency across ALL pages on your domain matters** — If Page A cites Page B as authoritative, and Page B contains inaccurate information, Page A loses citation credibility
- **Internal consistency is a new trust signal** — Contradictions between your own pages reduce AI Trust Score
- **Source chains are evaluated** — AI engines trace the authority chain: A cites B → B cites C; C's authority affects A's citation probability

**Implication:** Conduct internal consistency audits — do your own pages contradict each other on facts, statistics, or claims?

### Finding #6 — Perplexity Spaces: Multi-Agent Research Teams

Perplexity's "Spaces" feature represents a shift from single-agent to **multi-agent research collaboration**:
- Each Space contains specialized agents for different source types (web, academic, news, video)
- A single user query triggers multiple agents simultaneously
- Agents cross-reference and synthesize findings

**Content implications:**
- Content must be optimized for **domain-specific agent crawlers**, not just general web crawlers
- Academic/institutional sources get their own agent — scholarly content gets a dedicated discovery path
- Video content has its own agent — transcript + chapter markers are the optimization targets

### Finding #7 — Entity Authority Stacking: Transitive Trust in Citations

AI citation follows **transitive trust**: sources cited by authoritative sources accumulate citation credibility faster than isolated high-quality content.

The mechanism:
1. Authoritative source (A) cites content (B)
2. AI engine infers B has A's endorsement
3. B's citation probability increases independently of its own optimization
4. B can now be cited by other AI engines citing A's influence

**Practical implication:** Earn citations from the sources AI engines already trust (government portals, major academic institutions, recognized industry authorities) — these citations compound.

### Finding #9 — Semantic Depth vs. Surface Coverage

The shift from "hub and spoke" (broad topic coverage with many thin pages) to "deep dive" architecture (one comprehensive page per specific topic) is confirmed by AI selection patterns:

| Old Architecture | New Architecture | AI Selection Impact |
|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Hub page + 10 spoke thin pages | 10 comprehensive deep-dive pages | Deep dives get cited; hub pages rarely cited |
| Short definitions + "learn more" links | Complete explanations without external hops | AI prefers complete answers over partial + link model |
| FAQ sections as navigation | FAQ sections as citation targets | AI extracts FAQ answers directly |

### Finding #10 — AI Trust Score: Beyond Citation Rate

The **AI Trust Score** is a new content quality metric measuring:
- **Accuracy consistency**: Does this source provide correct information when re-verified?
- **Cross-page consistency**: Do all pages on this domain agree on shared facts?
- **Claim-to-source alignment**: Are the source's claims supported by the sources it cites?
- **Temporal accuracy**: Does the content remain accurate over time, or does it become outdated?

**High AI Trust Score benefits:**
- Cited more frequently in verification passes
- Included in multi-source AI answers (not just single-source answers)
- Recommended by Perplexity Spaces academic and institutional agents
- Less likely to be replaced by competitors in subsequent query generations
