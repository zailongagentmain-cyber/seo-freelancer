## Deep Dive: Finding #1 — March 2026 Core Update

- Follows the March 2026 Spam Update (March 24–25 — shortest confirmed spam update in history)

- SISTRIX Radar shows elevated movements across millions of SERPs

## Deep Dive: Finding #2 — AI Overviews Monetization

AI Overviews are now appearing for approximately **13% of all Google queries**, with ads displayed alongside **25.5%** of these AI Overview results — a massive increase from 2025.

### ChatGPT Ad Economy Explodes
- **ChatGPT has hit $100 million in ad revenue**
- Self-serve ad access opening in April 2026
- This signals AI platforms are becoming serious advertising channels
- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is no longer optional — it's mandatory

### What This Means for SEOs
- Traditional CTR metrics are declining (zero-click searches rising)
- Being cited inside AI Overviews/answers is the new "ranking #1"
- Key strategies: place insights at the top, use Q&A format, ensure business-grade clarity
- Backlinks losing relevance; brand mentions in trusted sources gaining importance

## Deep Dive: Finding #3 — MCP Protocol (97M Installs)

The **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** has reached **97 million installs**, solidifying its position as the foundational standard for agentic AI deployments.

### MCP's Rise
- Major AI providers now support MCP-compatible tooling
- 97M installs in record time — faster than any previous AI protocol adoption
- Enables AI agents to connect to external tools and data sources seamlessly

### Google-Agent UA
- Google officially announced the "Google-Agent" user agent for Project Mariner
- All Google-hosted agents now carry this UA string
- This is Google's official recognition of AI agents as legitimate web citizens

### llms.txt Evolution
- Beyond the initial llms.txt specification, a broader architecture is emerging
- This new architecture enables AI agents to better understand and navigate websites
- Publishers need to prepare structured, agent-readable content at scale
