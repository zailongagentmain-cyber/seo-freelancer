## Finding 1: Google Search Live Goes Global — Gemini 3.1 Flash Live Powers 200+ Country Rollout

### Details
Google announced the global expansion of **Search Live** on March 27, 2026, making real-time conversational voice and camera search available in over 200 countries and territories wherever AI Mode is supported. The rollout is powered by Google's new **Gemini 3.1 Flash Live** audio and voice model, which Google describes as delivering "even more natural and intuitive conversations." A key differentiator is the model's inherent multilingual capability — users worldwide can speak to Search in their preferred language, removing previous English-centric limitations. To access Search Live, users open the Google app on Android or iOS and tap the Live icon under the Search bar, then ask questions aloud to receive audio responses augmented with follow-up web links. This represents the most significant global expansion of Google's conversational AI search interface to date.

### Source
Search Engine Roundtable / Google Blog
**Date:** March 27, 2026
**Actionability Score: 8/10**
> Optimize for voice-first search queries in all languages, not just English. With 200+ countries now accessible, multilingual content with natural conversational phrasing becomes a priority. Ensure your structured data and entity markup works across languages and that FAQ-style content addresses voice query patterns.

## Finding 2: Bing Testing Rounded Corners on Short Videos — Visual SERP UI Evolution

### Details
Microsoft Bing is actively testing rounded corners on short videos and standard video thumbnails within the Bing search results interface. Previously, Bing displayed videos with squared-off edges — the new rounded corner treatment represents a subtle but meaningful visual redesign of how video content is presented in Bing's SERPs. This UI change may signal Bing's broader strategy to modernize its search results presentation, potentially improving visual hierarchy and click-through rates for video content. The timing is notable as both Google and Bing appear to be independently refining their video content display treatments, raising the question of whether a broader industry shift toward richer video SERP layouts is underway.

### Source
Search Engine Roundtable
**Date:** March 27, 2026

## Finding 7: Google Passkey Security Rolling Out for Google Ads Account Access

### Details
Google Ads is rolling out Passkey security for account access, representing a significant shift in how advertisers authenticate into their Google Ads accounts. Passkeys replace traditional passwords with cryptographic credentials tied to a user's device (phone, laptop), making accounts significantly more resistant to phishing and credential theft. This change has direct implications for SEO and PPC practitioners who manage multiple Google Ads accounts — team-wide passkey adoption will require coordination. Google is positioning this as a security improvement as AI-powered phishing attacks increase in sophistication.

### Source
PPC News Feed (Google Ads)
**Date:** March 27, 2026
**Actionability Score: 6/10**
> Begin planning for passkey adoption across your Google Ads accounts. Ensure your team devices are set up to support passkeys (FIDO2/WebAuthn compatible). Coordinate with IT/administrators to avoid account lockouts during the transition period.

## Finding 9: Whitespark's Guide to Unstructured Citations for AI Visibility

### Details
Whitespark released an updated guide on unstructured citations — mentions of business names, addresses, and phone numbers (NAP) on third-party websites that are not structured (i.e., not in directories or schema markup) — and their growing importance for AI visibility. As AI search systems like Google AI Mode and Bing's Copilot pull business information from unstructured web text, having consistent and comprehensive NAP mentions across the web becomes a Local SEO and GEO priority. The guide covers how to identify unstructured citation opportunities, which platforms matter most, and how to audit existing citations for consistency.

### Source
Whitespark
**Date:** March 27, 2026
**Actionability Score: 7/10**
> Audit your unstructured citation profile for accuracy and completeness. Focus on industry-specific platforms, local news sites, and community pages where NAP mentions occur naturally.
