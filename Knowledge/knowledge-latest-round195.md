## Deep Dive: Finding #1 — March 2026 Core Update

**Google initiated a global core algorithm update on March 27, 2026**, anticipated to take up to two weeks to fully roll out.

### Key Mechanisms
- **Gemini 4.0 Semantic Filter** — detects low-quality AI content through semantic coherence analysis
- **Information Gain Priority** — content that adds genuinely new information ranks higher; repetition of common knowledge is penalized
- **Comparative Value Assessment** — Google now evaluates content against competing pages, not just in isolation

### What This Means
- Generic/template AI content is rapidly devalued
- Original research, unique data, real-world experience = rewarded
- "People-first content" with clear structure, defined terms, and specific examples gets priority
- AI-mediated environments favor unambiguous language and logical organization

## Deep Dive: Finding #2-3 — GEO & Zero-Click

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
The focus has shifted from achieving #1 in traditional SERPs to becoming a **cited authority source for AI systems**.

Key tactics:
- **Structured data** — Schema markup helps AI parse and cite your content
- **FAQ format** — Direct question-answer pairs are frequently cited by AI
- **Author credentials** — Transparent authorship builds AI trust
- **Entity clarity** — Clear brand/person/entity signals improve AI citation rates
- **Quote-worthy content** — Unique claims with data sources get cited more

### Zero-Click Searches (60%)
With AI Overviews present, most searches end without a click. Strategy must adapt:
- Optimize for **AI citation** not just ranking
- Target **featured snippets** as AI overview sources
- Build **brand authority** so users remember you even without clicking

## Deep Dive: Finding #4 — E-E-A-T for All

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) is no longer just for YMYL niches — it now applies to **all competitive search queries**.

Practical application:
- Show **real credentials** and experience in content
- Build **author pages** with demonstrable expertise
- Earn **brand mentions** on authoritative publications
- Maintain **consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across web
- Display **trust signals** — reviews, testimonials, case studies
