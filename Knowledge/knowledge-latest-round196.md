## Trending Topics

### 1. March 2026 Google Core Update is Live
Google's March 2026 Core Update was announced on March 27, 2026, and is expected to run for approximately two weeks (until ~April 10). The update follows a brief March Spam Update and comes amid generally high SERP volatility. SISTRIX radar shows significant movements across both UK and US markets. This is the first core update since December 2025.

### 2. Google-Agent User Agent: The Agentic Web Has Arrived
Google officially added a new "Google-Agent" user agent for agents hosted on Google infrastructure (e.g., Project Mariner). This signals Google's formal entry into the agentic web era — where AI agents browse, interact with, and transact on websites on behalf of users. Marie Haynes calls this "the biggest mindset shift in SEO history." WebMCP (Web Model Context Protocol) now enables agents to use website functionality natively — filling forms, completing purchases — without simulating human browsing.

### 3. Google TurboQuant: Vector Search Revolution
Google published research on TurboQuant — a vector quantization technique that reduces AI processing memory requirements dramatically while maintaining accuracy. Key impact: near-zero time to build vector search indexes. This opens the door for more AI Overviews, more personalized AI results, near-instantaneous indexing, and major advances in AI agents operating in the physical world.

### 4. AI Overviews Expanding: More Breakings News, New Citation Design
AI Overviews are now appearing more frequently for breaking news queries, ranking above traditional Top Stories. Google is also testing a white background for AI Overview citations (ditching the blue tint). Bing is similarly advancing with AI-based Shopping Tab recommendations and larger product ad formats.

### 5. AI Agent Protocols: MCP, A2A, UCP, AG-UI
Google published a breakdown of critical AI protocols every SEO should understand:
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: Lets agents securely access your backend data
- **A2A (Agent2Agent)**: Enables bot-to-bot communication and transactions
- **UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol)**: Lets machines buy products directly from SERPs
- **AG-UI (Agent User Interaction)**: Middleware for streaming real-time AI data

### 6. ChatGPT Ads: $100M Revenue Milestone, Self-Serve Launching April
OpenAI's ChatGPT has hit $100 million in ad revenue and is opening self-serve ad access in April 2026. AI-generated answers are now showing ads. This positions ChatGPT as a serious advertising platform and signals that GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) now has direct paid amplification options.

### 7. llms.txt and Next-Gen AI Site Architecture
The llms.txt file was described as "step one" — the conversation has moved to full architectural thinking about how AI agents discover, crawl, and act on web content. SEOs are now planning for agent-first site structures including structured data for agents, API endpoints, and native agent interaction layers.

### 8. Google Search Console Bugs: Impression Spikes & Missing Indexing Data
GSC's performance report is experiencing bugs causing massive impression spikes when certain filters are applied. Crawl stats date selectors are also reported buggy, and BigQuery exports have been intermittently broken. Page indexing reports are missing chunks of data. These issues make it harder to accurately track rankings during the core update period.

## Key Insights

1. **The agentic web is not future — it's now.** With Google-Agent, WebMCP, and UCP rolling out, SEOs must think beyond human visitors. Sites need to be optimized for machine readers AND machine actors. *Source: [Marie Haynes - Google-Agent analysis](https://www.mariehaynes.com/why-googles-new-google-agent-is-the-biggest-mindset-shift-in-seo-history/)*

2. **TurboQuant could make real-time, personalized AI Overviews the default.** Instant indexing + compressed vector search = AI that knows about new content almost immediately. Publishers who publish frequently and with strong topical authority will benefit most. *Source: [Marie Haynes - TurboQuant analysis](https://www.mariehaynes.com/turboquant-has-the-potential-to-fundamentally-change-how-search-and-ai-works/)*

3. **Stop chasing Reddit and Wikipedia for AI citations — understand what AI actually uses.** Research shows AI recommendations are driven by different signals than traditional ranking factors. GEO requires understanding citation patterns in AI answers, not just backlinks. *Source: [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/reddit-wikipedia-what-drives-ai-recommendations-472580)*

4. **The March 2026 Core Update is still rolling out — don't make changes yet.** With the update running until ~April 10, ranking positions are still in flux. Wait for full rollout before diagnosing impact and making strategic changes. *Source: [SISTRIX - March 2026 Core Update](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/march-2026-core-update-radar-visibility-analysis-and-background/)*

5. **AI Mode and AI Overviews are converging into one experience.** Google's Nick Fox stated "Search is becoming AI Search, and the Gemini app is your personal assistant." AI Mode and AI Overviews are increasingly seen as the same product. *Source: [Marie Haynes - Liz Reid interview analysis](https://www.mariehaynes.com/5-things-i-learned-about-the-future-of-search-from-liz-reids-latest-interview/)*

6. **YouTube is testing AI-summarized video titles** — replacing human-written titles with AI-generated summaries. This could impact video SEO strategy significantly if rolled out broadly. *Source: [Search Engine Roundtable - Daily Recap March 30, 2026](https://www.seroundtable.com/recap-03-30-2026-41130.html)*

7. **Bing is advancing its AI shopping experience** with AI-based recommendation blocks at the top of Shopping Tab results and larger product ad formats. E-commerce sites should monitor Bing's evolving SERP layout. *Source: [Search Engine Roundtable - Bing Shopping AI](https://www.seroundtable.com/bing-shopping-ai-based-recommendations-41113.html)*

8. **"Opting out of AI Overviews" is officially a huge engineering project for Google.** Despite demand, Google says building a proper opt-out mechanism is complex. Sites cannot easily remove themselves from AI Overviews. *Source: [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overview-controls-huge-engineering-project-40938)*
