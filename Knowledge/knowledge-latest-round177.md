## Finding 1: Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance Report Adds Grounding Query-Page Mapping
**Details:** Bing expanded its AI Performance Report in Bing Webmaster Tools (now in public preview) with a new "Grounding Query-Page Mapping" feature. Users can now see which specific pages on their site are being cited for which grounding queries in AI-generated answers across Microsoft Copilot and partner integrations. One grounding query may map to multiple pages and vice versa. This is the first-ever SEO tool feature that gives publishers direct visibility into their AI citation data — showing citation counts, page-level activity, grounding queries, and visibility trends over time. Note: click data is NOT yet included, and API access is not yet available.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable / Microsoft Bing Blog
**Date:** March 24, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 9

## Finding 2: Bing Webmaster Guidelines Updated — Copilot, Grounding API, and Citation Eligibility Now Explicitly Documented
**Details:** Microsoft updated the Bing Webmaster Guidelines design and language. The intro now explicitly states the guidelines cover "Bing search experiences, Copilot, and grounding API results." New language covers eligibility for "indexing and ranking," "grounding results and citations," and "sustained visibility and qualified traffic." Failure to follow guidelines may result in "reduced eligibility for grounding experiences." This is the first time Bing has formally documented AI grounding as part of its official webmaster guidelines, giving SEOs explicit criteria to optimize for.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable / Bing Webmaster Guidelines
**Date:** February 26, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 8

## Finding 3: Google Adds New Supported Properties for Discussion Forum & QA Page Structured Data
**Details:** Google updated its developer documentation for DiscussionForum and QAPage structured data to add new supported properties. The update aims to provide "more clarity on comment thread structure to Google ingestion systems" and "prevents misinterpretations in our handling of forum and Q&A content." This is significant for forum sites (Stack Overflow, Reddit-style, niche communities) and Q&A sites (FAQ pages, Help centers). Additionally, Google officially documented — for the first time — that robots meta tags placed outside the HTML head (i.e., in the body section) are now respected by Google Search. Previously this was undocumented behavior.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable / Google Search Documentation
**Date:** March 25, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 8

## Finding 4: Google Tests "Skip Digging, Start Guided Research" CTA — New Web Guide Expansion Interface
**Details:** Google is testing a new SERP call-to-action that says "Skip digging, start guided research," which drives users from standard search results to a Web Guide-like interface organized by AI-generated topic clusters. This is an expansion of Google's Web Guide (rolled out in July 2025, expanded to All tab in December 2025). The new CTA represents a potential new SERP format that changes how users navigate search results — organized by AI-selected topics rather than traditional 10-blue-link rankings. SEO implications: content structure and topic clustering within pages may become more important than traditional keyword ranking.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable
**Date:** March 25, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 8

## Finding 5: Google Business Profile Tests "Reply to Reviews with AI" Feature
**Details:** Google is testing an AI-powered "Reply to reviews with AI" option within Google Business Profile dashboards. The feature uses Google AI to generate personalized reply drafts to customer reviews, labeled "Use Google AI to create personalised replies that build trust." Early testing shows it works for some businesses but is not yet available for all unanswered reviews. This represents Google's continued push to integrate AI into GBP management. Note: this is a GBP dashboard feature, not a Place Page attribute (which covers WhatsApp/LINE/KakaoTalk integration covered in previous topics).

## Finding 6: Google AI Mode Shows "Quick Results from the Web" Above the AI Answer
**Details:** Google AI Mode is testing a new "Quick results from the web" section that displays organic quick links (site names, favicons, titles) above the main AI-generated answer. These are organic links, not ads. This is a new organic SERP feature within AI Mode that may send direct clicks before the AI answer is displayed. It represents Google's ongoing experimentation with balancing organic traffic referral with AI answer delivery. Glenn Gabe noted this alongside the "Sponsored Stores" ad unit discovery in AI Mode.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable (Glenn Gabe)
**Date:** March 25, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 7

## Finding 7: Google Merchant Center Requires Grayed-Out Buy Buttons for Out-of-Stock Products
**Details:** Google Merchant Center updated its landing page requirements to mandate that products listed as out-of-stock must display a grayed-out (disabled) add-to-cart or buy button on the product page. Previously this was inconsistently enforced. This is a new technical requirement for e-commerce sites using Google Shopping Ads. Product pages with no buy button or active buy buttons for out-of-stock items may now be non-compliant. Affects Google Merchant Center feed requirements and landing page quality standards.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable / Google Merchant Center Documentation
**Date:** March 26, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 8

## Finding 8: Google Ads Retires Legacy Ad Format Policy Requirements
**Details:** Google retired several legacy ad format policy documents effective March 17, 2026. The retired policies include: Form ad requirements, Image quality requirements, Responsive ad requirements, and Text ad requirements. These policies were tied to ad formats no longer in active use, having "evolved into newer Google products." Ginny Marvin confirmed this has "zero impact on existing ads and campaigns" (Responsive Search Ads, Responsive Display Ads, etc. are unaffected). This is a policy cleanup, not a substantive change, but it reduces compliance complexity for advertisers.
**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable / Google Ads Liaison
**Date:** March 17, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 7

## Finding 9: EU Signals Imminent Decision on Google DMA Compliance — Potential AI Search Impact
**Details:** The EU is preparing a ruling on Google's compliance with the Digital Markets Act (DMA), with an announcement expected in the near future. This follows increased regulatory scrutiny of Google's AI features (including AI Mode and Search Live) under DMA's "gatekeeper" provisions. If Google is found non-compliant, it could face mandatory changes to how AI Overviews, AI Mode, and other AI features are displayed in EU markets — potentially reducing AI citation opportunities for publishers in Europe. Bloomberg and Reuters coverage indicates the decision is imminent as of late March 2026.
**Source:** Search Engine Land / Wall Street Journal / Reuters
**Date:** March 24, 2026
**Actionability Score:** 9

## Finding 10: Bing Tests New Rounded Corners on Short Video Thumbnails in Search Results
**Details:** Microsoft Bing is A/B testing a new visual design for video thumbnails in search results — changing from squared-off edges to rounded corners for both short videos and standard video results. This is a UI/UX test, not an algorithmic change. However, it signals Bing's increased focus on video content presentation as video discovery becomes more central to search. For SEOs, this underscores the growing importance of video thumbnails and video structured data for Bing rankings. The visual change makes video results more prominent and clickable in Bing SERPs.
