## Finding 1: Wikipedia Bans AI-Generated Content — Community Vote Approves Sweeping Restrictions
**Details:** English Wikipedia adopted a comprehensive ban on AI-generated content in March 2026, following a community vote that concluded March 20 with overwhelming support. The policy prohibits editors from using LLMs to generate or significantly rewrite article content. The ban exists because LLM-generated text frequently violates Wikipedia's core principles of verifiability and the prohibition on original research — AI text hallucinated facts not supported by reliable sources. There are two narrow exceptions: (1) basic copyedits of the editor's own work, provided there is human review and no new content is introduced; and (2) translations from other Wikipedia language editions, subject to specific translation guidelines and human verification. German Wikipedia implemented similar restrictions in February 2026.
**Source:** Search Engine Journal / Wikipedia

## Finding 3: University of Toronto Study — AI Search Overwhelmingly Prefers Earned Media Over Brand Content
**Details:** The University of Toronto published the first large-scale citation analysis across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude (September 2025). The most striking finding: AI search heavily favors earned media. In consumer electronics, AI cited third-party authoritative sources 92.1% of the time, versus Google's 54.1% for the same queries. In automotive, the figures were 81.9% versus 45.1%. This means it is not just how you write your own content — press coverage, independent product reviews, and industry publication mentions carry substantially more weight in AI responses than content on your own branded domain. Brands need earned media strategies as much as on-site SEO.
**Source:** University of Toronto via arXiv (study cited in Search Engine Journal)

## Finding 5: GEO-16 Framework Identifies 16 On-Page Quality Factors That Predict AI Citation Likelihood
**Details:** The GEO-16 framework (arXiv, September 2025) analyzed 1,702 real citations from Brave, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity, identifying 16 on-page quality factors that predict whether content gets cited in AI responses. The top three factors: metadata and freshness, semantic HTML, and structured data. This means technical on-page factors are as important as writing quality for AI visibility. Microsoft's guidance confirms this: descriptive H2/H3 headings help AI systems know where a complete idea starts and ends, Q&A formatting is native to AI extraction, and hiding key answers in tabs or expandable menus can cause AI to skip them entirely.

## Finding 7: Google Publishes AI Agent Protocols — WebMCP, A2A, UCP, A2UI, AG-UI Define the Agentic Web
**Details:** Google's latest developer documentation outlines five AI agent protocols that are reshaping how websites need to be built for machine-to-machine interaction. MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets agents securely access backend data. A2A (Agent2Agent) enables bot-to-bot communication and transactions. UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) lets a machine buy your product directly from the SERPs. A2UI (Agent to User Interface) auto-composes new visual layouts for users. AG-UI is middleware for streaming real-time AI data. WebMCP specifically lets agents use website functionality natively (e.g., auto-filling lead forms) rather than viewing pixels like humans do. These protocols represent a fundamental shift from optimizing for human clicks to optimizing for direct machine action.
**Source:** Google Developers Blog / Search Engine Journal
**Date:** March 2026
**Actionability Score:** 10

## Finding 8: 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors — Dynamic GBP Engagement Surpasses Static NAP as the Differentiator
**Details:** The 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors report (Whitespark) confirms that behavioral and engagement signals — posts, photos, clicks, calls, direction requests, and review cadence — are climbing fast in importance. Opening hours are now the No. 5 local pack ranking factor; an BrightLocal study of 50 businesses found rankings dropped when listed as closed. Review velocity (recency) matters as much as volume — a business earning 12 reviews over three months sends stronger signals than one earning the same 12 reviews over three years. GBP Posts are identified as the most underused freshness signal. Photos that are recently uploaded outperform stale imagery. The shift is from "set and forget" GBP management to treating it as a live engagement channel.
**Source:** Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors Report / Search Engine Journal
**Date:** March 2026

## Finding 9: Three CMS Platforms Now Control 73% of the Market — Default Technical SEO Settings Impact Millions
**Details:** WordPress, Shopify, and Wix now collectively control approximately 73% of the entire CMS market (W3Techs data, October 2025). WordPress has plateaued at 43.3% of all websites after two decades of growth (down from 43.2% peak). Shopify rose to 7.2% (a 929% increase over 10 years), while Squarespace reached 3.4%. Joomla and Drupal each fell approximately 80%. Individual SEO consultants have minimal web-wide impact compared to what CMS platform defaults accomplish. The implication: to meaningfully shift SEO outcomes at scale, practitioners need to influence WordPress, Shopify, or Wix default settings — not just individual client sites. Combined 2025 revenue for Shopify and Wix topped $13.5 billion, funding engineering teams whose default configurations govern technical SEO for millions of sites simultaneously.
