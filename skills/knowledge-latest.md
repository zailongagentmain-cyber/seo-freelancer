# SEO Knowledge Latest - March 2026

## Key Trends

### 1. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- Primary goal evolving from securing clicks to being cited/recommended by AI models
- Fastest-growing discipline in digital marketing
- Optimizing for: Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity

### 2. Zero-Click Searches & AI Overviews
- Users get answers directly from AI-generated summaries
- Need to optimize for visibility WITHIN AI responses
- Traditional CTR dropping when AI Overviews appear

### 3. Agentic Web & Conversational AI
- AI transitioning from "answer engine" to "action engine"
- AI can execute transactions, not just recommend
- Conversational search with longer, natural language queries becoming dominant

### 4. E-E-A-T Paramount
- AI systems prioritize credible, authoritative sources
- Strong brand presence across platforms crucial
- User-generated content from Reddit, YouTube highly valued

### 5. Evolved Content Strategy
- Human-AI Hybrid: AI for ideation/optimization, humans for creativity
- Shift from keywords to topics and intent
- Structured data and schema markup vital for machine readability

### 6. New Metrics
- AI citation frequency
- Share of voice in AI responses
- AI Overview appearance rates
- Multi-platform impressions

### 7. Omnichannel Authority
- Content must be discoverable across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, YouTube, emerging AI platforms
