## Research Summary

Researched March 2026 SEO trends focusing on **Zero-Click SEO** — a distinct topic from Round 99's Predictive SEO.

### Key Insights Discovered

1. **Scale of zero-click problem**: ~60-65% of searches result in no click in 2026; AI Overviews, featured snippets, Knowledge Panels are default responses
2. **High-quality traffic paradox**: Zero-click visitors are pre-qualified — those who DO click are more engaged and higher intent
3. **Three zones of SERP real estate**: Zone 1 (AI Overview), Zone 2 (Featured Elements), Zone 3 (Traditional Organic)
4. **Entity-based content architecture**: Search engines understand entities, not just keywords — structured entity relationships are key for AI citation
5. **Featured snippet optimization**: Q&A formatting, definition lists, table markup, numbered lists for process queries, clear content breaks
6. **Brand visibility without clicks**: AI citations build brand recognition even without click-throughs; measurable via brand mention tracking and direct navigation lift
7. **Converting post-zero-click visitors**: Immediate depth signals, acknowledgment framing, progressive disclosure, strong CTAs above fold
8. **New measurement framework**: AI Overview impression share, featured snippet win rate, zero-click conversion rate, engaged visitor rate
