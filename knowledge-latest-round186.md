## Finding 5: AI Overviews Now a Standard Feature — Optimization Is Mandatory
### Details
AI-generated summaries (AI Overviews in Google, AI answers in Bing) have become a standard, persistent feature in search results across both major engines in 2026. The shift from "answer engines" is complete — for informational queries, AI summaries are now the default experience. Optimizing for AI Overviews means structuring content for machine readability, demonstrating strong E-E-A-T, and providing "answer-ready" content that directly addresses user questions in the first paragraph or bullet point.
### Source
Conductor / Search Engine Journal / Marketing Milk
**Date:** 2026 (ongoing)
**Actionability Score:** 9
> Implement the "Question → Answer → Expand" content framework; front-load answers and use clear structure (bullets, numbered lists, FAQs) to get cited in AI Overviews.

## Finding 6: E-E-A-T Is Non-Negotiable — Human Content Outperforms Generic AI Output
### Details
In 2026, E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is applied to virtually all competitive searches, not just YMYL topics. Google's algorithms are increasingly adept at detecting low-quality AI-generated content. Content with original insights, first-hand experiences, verifiable facts, and a clear author identity consistently outranks generic, heavily AI-edited material. Google's guidance explicitly rewards content that is "clear, reliable, well-organized, and answers central questions early."
### Source
Google Search Central / Envision IT Agency / Marketer Milk
**Date:** 2026 (ongoing)
**Actionability Score:** 9
> Audit your content for genuine expertise signals: author bios, citations, original research, and real-world experience — generic AI content will be devalued.

## Finding 8: Technical SEO Foundations Remain Critical — Core Web Vitals & Schema
### Details
Despite the AI-first shift, foundational technical SEO — page speed, Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP), mobile-first indexing, HTTPS, clean site architecture, XML sitemaps, and structured data/schema markup — remains essential for AI systems to crawl, index, and extract information from content. Schema types including Article, FAQ, HowTo, and Dataset markup are increasingly important for AI Overview eligibility and citation.
### Source
Botify / Envision IT Agency / Marketing Aid / Conductor
**Date:** 2026 (ongoing)
**Actionability Score:** 8
> Run a Core Web Vitals audit and implement FAQ + Article schema markup — technical health directly gates AI visibility.
