## 什么是GEO？| What is GEO?

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** 是针对AI搜索结果的优化策略。随着ChatGPT、Perplexity、Claude等AI助手成为用户获取信息的新入口，传统的SEO正在进化为GEO。

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is an optimization strategy for AI search results. As AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude become new entry points for users to obtain information, traditional SEO is evolving into GEO.

---

## GEO vs 传统SEO | GEO vs Traditional SEO

| 维度 | 传统SEO | GEO |
|------|---------|-----|
| Target | Google排名 | AI引用/摘要 |
| Core | 关键词、外链 | 权威性、可引用性 |
| Form | 10个蓝色链接 | 直接答案 |
| Key Metric | CTR | 引用率 |

## 5个GEO核心策略 | 5 Core GEO Strategies

### 1. 引用优化 Citation Optimization
- 使用结构化数据标记关键信息
- Create structured data to mark key information
- 创建可直接引用的简明答案
- Create concise answers that can be directly cited
- 在内容中使用事实性陈述
- Use factual statements in content

### 2. E-E-A-T 强化 | Strengthen E-E-A-T
- **Experience**: 展示第一手经验 | Showcase first-hand experience
- **Expertise**: 展示专业背景 | Display professional background
- **Authoritativeness**: 建立行业权威 | Build industry authority
- **Trustworthiness**: 增加信任信号 | Increase trust signals

### 3. 语义搜索优化 | Semantic Search Optimization
- 覆盖主题的多个角度 | Cover multiple angles of the topic
- 使用自然语言而非关键词堆砌 | Use natural language instead of keyword stuffing
- 回答用户潜在问题 | Answer potential user questions

### 4. 数字权威建设 | Digital Authority Building
- 获取媒体报道而非单纯外链 | Get media coverage instead of just backlinks
- 建立 Thought Leadership | Establish Thought Leadership
- 参与行业对话 | Participate in industry conversations

### 5. AI平台优化 | AI Platform Optimization
- Perplexity: 结构化内容优先 | Prioritize structured content
- ChatGPT Search: 直接回答问题 | Answer questions directly
- 使用对话式标题 | Use conversational titles
