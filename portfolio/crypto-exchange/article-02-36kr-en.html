My Recommendation

Choose Binance if:

  • You're an active trader who cares about fees
  • You want access to obscure altcoins
  • You're comfortable with a more complex interface

Choose Coinbase if:

  • You're new to crypto and want a simple experience
  • You're in the US and need regulatory peace of mind
  • You want easy fiat on/off ramps

Choose Kraken if:

  • Security is your #1 priority
  • You want competitive fees without Binance's regulatory risk
  • You value transparency and proof of reserves

The Smart Strategy

Most experienced traders use multiple exchanges:

  • Binance or Bybit for low fees and altcoin trading
  • Kraken for holding long-term positions securely
  • Crypto.com for spending with their card

What About Decentralized Exchanges?

DEXs like Uniswap are great for privacy and DeFi, but they lack fiat on/off ramps, have higher technical barriers, and sometimes worse liquidity for large trades.

Best for: Experienced DeFi users who want to keep keys in their own wallets.

The Bottom Line

There's no perfect exchange. The "best" one depends entirely on your priorities: fees vs. security vs. simplicity vs. regulatory clarity.

My advice: Start with one that's licensed in your country, deposit a small amount, test the withdrawal process, then optimize from there.
