## Google Business Profile Optimization 2026

### 1. Complete Every Field

Your GBP profile should be 100% complete:

```
- Business name (exact match to signage)
- Address (accurate, no abbreviations)
- Phone (local number, toll-free avoided)
- Website (optimized, mobile-first)
- Hours (including special hours)
- Categories (primary + secondary)
- Attributes (most relevant)
- Products/Services (detailed)
```

### 2. AI-Optimized Business Description

Your description should:
- Include primary keywords naturally
- Describe what makes you unique
- Mention service areas
- Be 750+ characters
- Update regularly for AI freshness

Example:
```
✅ "Premier SEO agency in San Francisco helping local businesses dominate search results since 2015. Specializing in local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, and AI search visibility. Serving Bay Area clients with proven strategies."
```

### 3. Photo & Video Strategy

AI prioritizes businesses with:
- Regular photo updates (weekly)
- Professional interior/exterior shots
- Team photos (humanize your brand)
- Before/after photos
- Short video tours (15-30 seconds)
- Customer experience photos

### 4. Q&A Optimization

Populate and monitor Q&A:
- Pre-populate with common questions
- Use keywords naturally in answers
- Respond to all user questions quickly
- Add 5-10 strategic Q&As

### 5. Posts Strategy

Regular posts improve AI signals:
- Weekly updates minimum
- Use all post types (Update, Offer, Event)
- Include CTAs
- Add relevant keywords
- Post about products/services

## Local Content Optimization

### 1. Service Area Pages

Create dedicated pages for each service area:

```
Structure:
- H1: [Service] in [City]
- Introduction: Local intent + keywords
- Local examples: Case studies
- Reviews: Client testimonials
- CTA: Contact form
```

### 2. Location Pages

If you have multiple locations:
- Unique content per location
- Local keywords in content
- Embed Google Maps
- List hours specific to location
- Include local phone number

### 3. Local Keyword Research

Target keywords:
- "[Service] + [location]"
- "best [service] in [city]"
- "[service] near me"
- "[service] [city] [neighborhood]"
- Questions: "where to get [service] in [city]"

### 4. Review Management

Reviews are crucial for AI ranking:
- Encourage reviews after service
- Respond to ALL reviews
- Use keywords naturally in responses
- Address negative reviews professionally
- Showcase reviews on website

## Technical Local SEO

### 1. NAP Consistency

Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) is consistent everywhere:

```
Check:
- Website
- GBP
- Social media
- Local directories
- Citation sites
- Press releases
```

### 2. Schema Markup

Add local business schema:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Business Name",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "123 Main St",
    "addressLocality": "City",
    "addressRegion": "State",
    "postalCode": "12345"
  },
  "telephone": "+1-555-123-4567",
  "openingHours": "Mo-Fr 09:00-18:00",
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 37.7749,
    "longitude": -122.4194
  }
}
```

### 3. Mobile Optimization

Local search is mobile-first:
- Fast loading (under 3 seconds)
- Easy-to-tap phone numbers
- Click-to-call functionality
- Simple contact forms
- Clear directions link
