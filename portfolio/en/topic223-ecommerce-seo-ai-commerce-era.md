## The New Ecommerce Search Surface

The intersection of ecommerce and AI search is no longer a forward-looking concept — it is an active, live advertising and discovery surface that is reshaping how products get found, evaluated, and purchased. Google Loyalty programs are now running inside AI Mode and Gemini across 14 countries. Performance Max has received audience exclusions and granular placement reporting. Shopping Ads are expanding into 15 new European markets. And in the background, the fundamental question every ecommerce SEO professional needs to answer is shifting: not "how do we rank on the SERP?" but "how do we win on the AI commerce surface?"

The 11 findings in this round cover the operational realities of this new surface — feed compliance requirements that are now enforced with automatic disapprovals, the behavioral ranking signals that are transforming local product discovery, the click-friction dynamics that AI Mode introduces into branded product mentions, and the expanding ad surfaces where ecommerce brands need to be present.

## Finding 1: GMC Mandates Grayed-Out Buy Buttons for Out-of-Stock Products — Enforced April 2026

Google Merchant Center has updated its landing page requirements with an enforcement date in April 2026: product pages displaying out-of-stock items must now feature a grayed-out "add to cart" or "buy" button rather than hiding the button entirely or showing an active purchase button for unavailable inventory.

This is a policy requirement, not a recommendation. Sites that fail to comply risk having their product feeds disapproved or their Shopping Ads paused. The enforcement mechanism is automatic: Google's systems will detect non-compliant product pages and trigger feed disapprovals without manual review.

The strategic importance: for ecommerce sites with large catalogs, out-of-stock fluctuations are constant. A single category-wide stock-out without grayed-out buttons can trigger mass feed disapprovals, shutting off Shopping Ads for the entire catalog — not just the out-of-stock SKUs.

**Your action this week:** Audit your top 10 product pages for out-of-stock variants. Verify that each displays a grayed-out purchase button. Automate this as a template-level rule in your product page infrastructure so any new out-of-stock SKU automatically receives compliant treatment.

## Finding 2: Google Loyalty Program Now Live in AI Mode and Gemini — 14 Countries

Google has launched its Loyalty Program integration inside AI Mode and Gemini across 14 countries — a direct monetization play that connects loyalty program membership to AI-generated product recommendations and purchase pathways.

For ecommerce brands, this is a new product ranking surface with direct conversion potential. Users who are logged into loyalty accounts and have linked those accounts to Google's AI systems will receive personalized product recommendations that factor in their loyalty program status, points balance, and purchase history. Being the featured product in an AI Mode loyalty recommendation is the equivalent of ranking #1 for a high-intent branded query — with the added benefit of a direct loyalty-program-linked purchase pathway.

The competitive implication: brands without loyalty programs are already disadvantaged on this surface. Brands with loyalty programs that have not integrated with Google's loyalty partnership infrastructure are leaving a live commerce channel unaddressed.

**Your action this week:** Determine whether your ecommerce brand has a loyalty or rewards program. If yes, investigate whether it is integrated with Google's loyalty partnership ecosystem. If not, evaluate the investment required to build or enhance a loyalty program as a prerequisite for AI Mode visibility.

## Finding 3: Performance Max Gets Audience Exclusions and Granular Placement Reporting

Google has delivered two significant upgrades to Performance Max campaigns that ecommerce advertisers have requested for years: audience exclusion controls and granular placement reporting within the AI surfaces where PMax distributes ads.

Audience exclusions in PMax mean that ecommerce advertisers can now prevent their AI-driven ad delivery from targeting or showing ads to specific audience segments — for example, excluding past purchasers from product catalog campaigns where the goal is new customer acquisition. Previously, PMax's AI would automatically optimize across all available inventory, including segments advertisers explicitly did not want to spend against.

Granular placement reporting means that PMax reporting now shows exactly which surfaces — AI Mode, traditional Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display — are generating conversions, enabling more precise budget allocation decisions.

**Your action this week:** Review your current PMax campaign structure. If you are running catalog campaigns without audience exclusions, you are likely wasting budget on segments that cannot convert. Add audience exclusions and review the new placement reporting to reallocate budget toward the highest-performing surfaces.

## Finding 4: Shopping Ads Expanding to 15 New European Markets

Google is expanding Shopping Ads availability to 15 new European markets — a significant geographic expansion that creates new ecommerce advertising opportunities across the continent. For brands with European fulfillment capabilities, this is a direct signal to activate or expand Google Merchant Center feeds for the new markets.

The expansion follows the completion of Google's European market-by-market shopping product policy harmonization, meaning the feed compliance requirements are now standardized across the expansion markets rather than varying by country as they previously did.

**Your action this week:** Identify whether any of the 15 new European markets are within your fulfillment footprint. If you ship to those countries, activate Google Merchant Center feeds for those markets immediately to be early in the competitive landscape for Shopping Ads in new territories.

## Finding 5: Vehicle Ads Data Quality Mismatches Trigger automatic Disapproval

Google's Vehicle Ads product — which serves automotive dealer inventory ads across Google's surfaces — has implemented automatic disapproval triggers for data quality mismatches between the submitted inventory feed and the landing page. If the feed says a vehicle is in stock but the landing page returns a 404, or if pricing in the feed does not match the landing page price, the specific vehicle listing is automatically disapproved.

This is a precursor to similar automated enforcement mechanisms that Google will likely extend to general product Shopping Ads. The mechanism demonstrates Google's direction of travel: not manual review and warning periods, but automated quality enforcement that treats feed-to-page consistency as a non-negotiable requirement.

**Your action this week:** If you run Vehicle Ads or manage a product catalog feed: run a feed-to-page consistency audit. Verify that every live SKU in your feed resolves to a correct landing page with matching pricing and availability information. Any mismatch is now an automatic disapproval trigger, not a warning.

## Finding 6: AI Mode Overlay Cards Add Click-Friction to Branded Product Mentions

Google is actively testing overlay card link treatments in AI Mode: when a brand or product is mentioned in an AI Mode response, the traditional hyperlink is replaced with an overlay card that requires an additional click to reach the brand's site. What was previously a direct click now requires two interactions.

This follows the pattern documented in earlier rounds: AI Mode is architecturally designed to keep users within Google's environment. The overlay card treatment applies to branded mentions, meaning brands that are cited or recommended in AI Mode responses may receive fewer direct referral clicks than the citation would have generated in a traditional SERP context.

For ecommerce brands, the implication is direct: the AI Mode citation is valuable for brand visibility and purchase consideration, but the conversion funnel now has an additional friction step. Brands should ensure that their AI Mode citations — whether organic or from Shopping Ads — are optimized for the two-click journey: clear brand messaging within the overlay card, and a friction-minimized landing page experience for the second click.

**Your action this week:** Monitor your branded referral traffic from AI Mode surfaces in Google Search Console. If you see a pattern of impressions with disproportionately low clicks from AI Mode, your brand may be experiencing overlay card treatment. Audit your brand's AI Mode citation content for clarity and call-to-action strength.

## Finding 7: AI Overviews Distribution Varies Starkly by Query Type — 6% for News vs. 60%+ for Health

Research presented in March 2026 revealed the stark variation in AI Overview distribution across query categories: breaking news queries show AI Overview appearances in fewer than 6% of results, while health-related queries show AI Overview presence in more than 60% of results. The range between lowest and highest categories spans a full order of magnitude.

For ecommerce SEO, the practical implication is the query category matters more than the product type. Product queries that map to informational intent ("best running shoes for flat feet," "how to choose a mattress firmness") are far more likely to trigger AI Overviews than transactional queries ("buy Nike Air Max size 10"). Ecommerce content that includes strong informational components — buying guides, product comparison frameworks, condition-specific recommendations — is more likely to appear in AI Overviews than thin transactional product pages.

**Your action this week:** Map your top product category queries by intent type. For informational-intent categories, ensure your content is structured to be cited in AI Overviews — front-loaded answers, clear comparative frameworks, authoritative authorship signals. For transactional categories, optimize for direct conversion pathways rather than AI Overview citations.

## Finding 8: GBP Performance Dashboard Now Tracks Offer Views and Clicks

Google Business Profiles for retail locations have received a significant upgrade to their performance reporting: the dashboard now tracks offer views and clicks directly, not just direction requests and phone calls. This means that for brands with physical retail locations, the performance of GBP-hosted promotional offers — seasonal sales, in-store discounts, loyalty program offers — can now be measured directly against GBP performance.

The reporting upgrade reflects the broader shift in local search: GBP has evolved from a static directory listing to a live engagement surface. Offers, posts, product inventories, and real-time hours are all active ranking and engagement signals. The new reporting makes it possible to close the loop between GBP offer investment and in-store or website conversion.

**Your action this week:** If you have physical retail locations, review the new GBP offer reporting. Identify which offer types — discount codes, loyalty rewards, seasonal promotions — generate the highest offer views and click-through rates. Use this data to prioritize offer investment in the highest-performing promotional types.

## Finding 9: Shopping Ads Political Content Policy Requires Election Verification by April 16

Google has introduced a new Shopping Ads policy covering political content that requires election verification by April 16, 2026. Any ecommerce advertiser running Shopping Ads that touch political content categories — campaign merchandise, political publications, issue-based products — must complete Google's political advertising verification process before running those campaigns.

This is distinct from Google's existing political advertising policies for Search and YouTube; the new Shopping Ads policy creates a specific verification requirement for product-based political content. The enforcement deadline of April 16, 2026 means that any non-compliant political product Shopping Ads will be suspended on that date.

**Your action this week:** If your product catalog includes any items that could be classified as political content — campaign merchandise, political books or publications, issue-based products — audit them against Google's new policy requirements. Initiate the verification process before April 16 to avoid campaign suspension.

## Finding 10: Legacy Google Ads Format Requirements Retired (March 17, 2026)

Google officially retired legacy Google Ads format requirements on March 17, 2026 — simplifying the creative approval framework and removing several historical restrictions on ad copy length, headline count, and description formatting that dated back to the pre-Responsive Search Ad era.

The practical effect: advertisers have more flexibility in how they structure ad creative within Google's AI-driven optimization system. The retirement of legacy format requirements is consistent with Google's broader shift toward AI-optimized ad delivery, where the system determines optimal creative combinations based on performance data rather than enforcing fixed format rules.

For ecommerce advertisers, the retirement removes constraints on how product feeds and ad creative are combined — particularly relevant for Shopping Ads where feed data drives the ad display.

**Your action this week:** Review your current Google Ads creative strategy. With legacy format constraints removed, experiment with more varied headline and description combinations. For Shopping Ads specifically, ensure your product feed data is structured to give Google's AI the maximum creative flexibility to generate high-performing ad combinations.

## Finding 11: GBP Now Supports WhatsApp, LINE, and KakaoTalk Chat — Messaging Diversity in Local Search

Google Business Profiles now support WhatsApp, LINE, and KakaoTalk chat integration as contact options within GBP — responding to the regional messaging platform preferences that dominate in key ecommerce markets across Asia and Latin America.

For brands with local retail presence in markets where these messaging platforms are dominant — WhatsApp in Brazil and much of Southeast Asia, LINE in Japan and Thailand, KakaoTalk in South Korea — the addition of these contact options to GBP transforms the profile from a directory listing into a multi-channel customer engagement surface. Users can now initiate WhatsApp, LINE, or KakaoTalk conversations directly from the GBP listing without leaving Google's environment.

**Your action this week:** Identify which of these messaging platforms are dominant in your key retail markets. If your target markets include Japan, Thailand, South Korea, Brazil, or Southeast Asia, add the relevant messaging options to your GBP immediately. Ensure the messaging accounts are actively monitored — a GBP listing that offers WhatsApp chat but does not respond is worse than no chat option at all.
