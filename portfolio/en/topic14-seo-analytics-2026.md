- Unique visitors
- Total pageviews
- Pages per session

- Organic vs. paid vs. direct
- Referral traffic
- Social traffic

#### Keyword Rankings
- Position tracking
- SERP features captured
- Ranking velocity
- Competitor comparison

#### Visibility Score

- Click-through potential
- Share of voice

### 3. Engagement Metrics

#### Bounce Rate
- Single-page sessions
- Benchmark by industry
- Trend analysis

#### Dwell Time
- Time on page

- Correlation with rankings

#### Pages per Session
- User flow depth
- Content engagement
- Internal linking effectiveness

### 4. Conversion Metrics

#### Goal Completions
- Form submissions
- Newsletter signups
- Purchases
- Downloads

#### Conversion Rate
- Organic conversion rate
- Revenue per session
- Value per visitor

## Advanced SEO Analytics

### 1. Core Web Vitals

Track these vital metrics:

| Metric | Good | Needs Improvement | Poor |
|--------|------|------------------|------|
| LCP | <2.5s | 2.5-4s | >4s |
| INP | <200ms | 200-500ms | >500ms |
| CLS | <0.1 | 0.1-0.25 | >0.25 |

### 2. Search Console Metrics

- Trend analysis

#### Clicks
- Total clicks
- CTR by query
- CTR by position

#### Average Position

- Position trends

### 3. Brand Metrics

- Brand search volume
- Brand mentions
- Sentiment analysis
- Share of voice

## Measuring SEO ROI

### Calculate Your SEO ROI

```
ROI = (Revenue from SEO - Cost of SEO) / Cost of SEO × 100%
```

### Track Revenue

- E-commerce conversions
- Lead generation
- Brand awareness value
- Customer lifetime value

### Measure Costs

- Tools and software
- Content creation

## Common Analytics Mistakes

1. **Tracking vanity metrics**: Focus on actionable data
2. **Ignoring trends**: Track over time, not snapshots
3. **No benchmarks**: Compare to industry standards
4. **Missing context**: Correlate with business metrics
5. **Data silos**: Integrate across platforms
6. **No reporting**: Regular reports drive decisions
