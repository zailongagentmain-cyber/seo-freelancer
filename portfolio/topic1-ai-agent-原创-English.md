# AI Agent: The Most Promising Tech Revolution of 2025

## What is an AI Agent?

An AI Agent is an autonomous system capable of completing complex tasks independently. Unlike traditional chatbots, AI Agents don't just answer questions—they take actions, make decisions, and learn from interactions.
