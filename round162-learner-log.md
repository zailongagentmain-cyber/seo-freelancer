## Key Insights This Round

### Most Actionable
- **Finding 1 (CWV = quality filter):** The shift from tiebreaker to filter is a material change — SEOs who ignored CWV can no longer use "our content is good enough" as a defense. Immediate audit recommended.
- **Finding 2 (GBP completeness = ranking factor):** The explicit scoring of GBP completeness means missing any element (photos, Q&A, attributes, service lists) now has algorithmic consequences, not just UX consequences.

### Most Surprising
- **Finding 3 (Review disappearance bug):** The scale ("hundreds of millions" of reviews removed) and the fact that Google publicly acknowledged it is notable. The takeaway isn't fear — it's the reinforcement that recent reviews outweigh legacy volume.
- **Finding 7 (AI-generated content without human experience):** Confirms a trend that has been building for months but is now a confirmed algorithmic signal, not just speculation.

### Most Nuanced
- **Finding 8 (AI Overview traffic quality):** The finding that AI referral traffic actually converts at higher rates than average organic traffic is counterintuitive given the CTR anxiety around AI Overviews. Worth communicating to clients as a "don't panic" data point.
