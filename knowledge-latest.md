## Finding 5: AI Mode Link UI Change Reduces Direct Clicks — Overlay Cards Tested

**Details:** Google is testing overlay cards (bubble links) in AI Mode that replace direct links to websites. Instead of clicking through to a brand's site when it's mentioned, users must click again to visit. This will measurably reduce direct referral traffic from AI Mode citations. Brodie Clark documented the test. For SEOs, this is a signal that even when you rank well in AI Mode, you may get fewer clicks. Focus on earning citations in AI Overviews AND ensuring your brand presence is so strong that users feel compelled to click despite the extra step.
