## Deep Dive: Finding #1 — Post-Update Recovery: 90-Day Playbook

Following the March 2026 Core Update (~80% complete as of March 31), sites that were impacted now face a **90-day recovery window** — the standard period Google allows before assessing recovery attempts.

### The 90-Day Rule
- Google typically waits 90 days after a core update before evaluating whether a site's changes have had positive impact
- **Do NOT make major changes expecting instant recovery** — Google's systems need time to re-crawl and reassess
- Premature changes can actually hurt recovery by disrupting ranking signals mid-evaluation

### Recovery Action Plan (Priority Order)
1. **Audit E-E-A-T signals first** — Author credentials, experience documentation, about page depth
2. **Identify thin content clusters** — Pages with <400 words, no unique data, purely affiliate
3. **Add original research** — Surveys, case studies, proprietary data that competitors cannot replicate
4. **Improve content depth** — Target 2,000+ words with genuine insight, not padding
5. **Fix technical issues** — Core Web Vitals, crawl errors, mobile usability
6. **Remove or redirect toxic links** — Use Google Reconsider Requests only after 90 days

### Sites That Should NOT Expect Full Recovery
- Pure AI-generated thin content at scale
- Sites with deceptive practices (cloaking, link schemes)
- Content that was helpful before but is now outpaced by better resources

## Deep Dive: Finding #2 — Entity Authority > Keyword Authority

The March 2026 Core Update has accelerated Google's shift toward **entity-based understanding**. Keywords are giving way to **things** (entities) and **context**.

### What Entity SEO Means Practically
- Google no longer just matches keywords — it understands *what your content is about* in a semantic network
- **Knowledge Graph entities** must be explicitly connected to your content via structured data
- Author entities must be linked to their expertise domain in the Knowledge Graph

### Entity SEO Technical Stack
1. **Organization Schema** — Full organizational details (name, logo, contact, sameAs social links)
2. **Person Schema** — Author credentials, job title, area of expertise linked to sameAs Wikipedia/LinkedIn
3. **Article Schema** — Article structured data with author reference to Person entity
4. **FAQ Schema** — Q&A blocks that train AI on your entity's expertise domain
5. **Speakable Schema** — Voice-search optimized sections (AIO-friendly)
6. **Book/Product/Review Schema** — Domain-specific entity connections

### The Brand-as-Entity Shift
- Strong brands = strong entities = more trust signals in AI-generated answers
- Anonymous or generic content sites struggle to build entity authority
- **Invest in brand building** — press mentions, industry awards, professional associations = entity authority

## Deep Dive: Finding #3 — Multi-Platform GEO: ChatGPT vs Perplexity vs Bing AI

Each AI search platform has distinct citation preferences. A unified GEO strategy requires understanding platform-specific differences.

### Platform Citation Comparison

| Platform | Citation Style | Preferred Content | Tracking Method |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| ChatGPT (OpenAI) | Inline paragraph citation | Long-form authoritative, primary sources | Bing Webmaster + third-party tools |
| Perplexity | Numbered footnote citations | Concise answers, data-backed claims | Perplexity Publisher API |
| Bing AI | Inline text links | Conversational, question-focused | Bing Webmaster Tools |
| Google AIO | No direct citation | Schema-rich, direct answers | Google Search Console |
| Claude (Anthropic) | No public citations | Factual, encyclopedic content | Anthropic partner program (invite only) |

### Multi-Platform GEO Tactics
1. **Submit to Bing Webmaster** — Bing powers ChatGPT's web citations; Bing indexing = ChatGPT citation
2. **Perplexity-specific optimizations** — Short, direct answers + source links + "About" page quality
3. **Bing AI content** — Conversational tone, FAQ format, Q&A with clear answers in first sentence
4. **YouTube transcript optimization** — AI agents transcribe videos; well-structured chapters = higher citation chance

### ChatGPT Search GEO Strategy
- ChatGPT uses Bing's index + its own analysis
- Bing Webmaster Tools submission is a prerequisite
- Cited sources tend to be: established news sites, high-authority blogs, academic/institutional content
- Content with strong author bylines gets preference

## Deep Dive: Finding #5 — Answer Engine Density (AED): The New Content Metric

**Answer Engine Density (AED)** is emerging as the replacement for traditional word count as an AI-content-quality metric.

### What is AED?
- AED measures how many **directly answerable questions** your content addresses within the first 200 words
- AI engines (AIOs, Perplexity) extract answers from top-of-content positions
- Higher AED = higher chance of being cited in AI answers

### AED Optimization Formula
1. **Lead with the answer** — Put the main conclusion in the first 2-3 sentences
2. **Question-intent headings** — H2s/H3s phrased as questions users actually ask
3. **Direct answer sentences** — Each paragraph starts with a complete sentence that answers the heading question
4. **FAQ-dense content** — 5-10 FAQ questions with concise 1-2 sentence answers
5. **Schema markup** — JSON-LD FAQPage + SpeakableSpecification

### AED vs Word Count
| Traditional SEO | Answer Engine SEO |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Word count: 2,000+ | AED score: 8+ answers in first 200 words |
| Keyword density | Entity覆盖率 |
| Internal links | Cross-platform citations |
| Meta description | Featured snippet targeting |

### Zero-Click GEO: 7 Strategies
1. **Position zero isn't dead** — AIOs often cite featured snippet text verbatim
2. **Wikipedia method** — Structure content like encyclopedia entries for AI training data alignment
3. **The "cited by" strategy** — Link to and cite authoritative sources; AI trusts content that cites trusted sources
4. **Contrarian positions** — AI rewards confident, evidence-backed disagreements with consensus
5. **Data visualization** — Charts, tables, and infographics get cited in AI answers more than plain text
6. **Source transparency** — Clearly cite dates, methodology, sample sizes for research claims
7. **FAQ dominance** — Owning the FAQ for a topic = owning the AI answer for that topic

### YouTube Chapters + Timestamps for AI
- AI agents transcribe and parse YouTube videos
- Well-structured chapters (with keyword-rich titles) = higher video citation
- Timestamps in video descriptions help AI extract specific answers
- YouTube SEO now intersects with GEO — video content is cited in text-based AI answers
