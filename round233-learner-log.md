## Topic Selection Rationale

Topic 271's theme emerged from a specific observation: the prior rounds covered the incoming wave (agentic crawling, AI surfaces, algorithm updates). Topic 271 covers what's already broken on the other side of those waves — concrete, operational changes Google has made to how local commerce, product discovery, and e-commerce conversion paths work.

The four structural consequence areas:
1. **Local commerce** — call button collapse, GBP chat attributes as replacement
2. **Product feed compliance** — GMC out-of-stock mandate as regulatory-quality requirement
3. **SERP feature removal** — "What People Suggest" gone; Reddit/social signals declining on SERPs
4. **Commerce-SEO boundary blur** — Bing AI Shopping, Google Shopping Store Tab, live sports in AI Mode
